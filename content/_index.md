## Softness is a ritual.

Makhmal means velvet. It is the feeling we want your skin to have at the end of every day. Our gentle skincare routines protect your skin barrier, hold on to moisture, and stay simple enough to do every single day.

No twelve step pressure. No miracle promises. Just honest skincare that works.

---

## The Makhmal way

**Gentle first.** Healthy skin starts with a strong skin barrier. We choose soothing creams and hydrating serums over harsh scrubs and strong acids.

**Honest ingredients.** Every formula is written in plain language. You will always know what is in the jar and why it is there.

**Ritual over routine.** Two calm minutes in the morning and two at night will do more for your skin than a shelf full of products you never use.
