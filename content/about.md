Makhmal means velvet in Persian, Urdu, and Arabic. It is the word a grandmother uses for a baby's cheek. It felt like the right name for the way skincare should feel.

Makhmal Skin began with a simple frustration. Skincare had become loud. Ten step routines, confusing ingredient labels, and promises that never quite came true. We wanted the opposite. A quiet shelf of gentle products that simply work.

## What we believe

- Skin is not a problem to solve. It is something to look after.
- Fewer, better products. A small daily skincare routine beats a crowded shelf.
- Honesty builds trust. Clear labels and real expectations matter as much as any ingredient.
