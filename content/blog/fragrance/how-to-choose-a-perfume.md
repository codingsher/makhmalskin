Most unworn perfumes were bought in a hurry. Choosing a fragrance you will actually reach for takes a little patience and a lot less money than buying three you will not.

## Test on skin, never just paper

Paper strips show you the opening. Your skin shows you the truth. Spray one wrist, go about your day, and smell it again after an hour. That drydown is what you will actually live with.

## Learn your notes in plain language

Top notes are the first ten minutes. Heart notes are the afternoon. Base notes are what your scarf smells like tomorrow. If you love vanilla, sandalwood, or musk, you love base notes, so read the bottom of the pyramid first.

## One new perfume at a time

Testing five fragrances at once teaches you nothing. Wear one sample for a few days. If you finish the sample and miss it, that is your answer.

## Buy the small bottle

A scent you adore in a small bottle beats a bargain in a big one. Perfume also ages once opened, so small and loved is the smart buy.

The right perfume feels less like a purchase and more like a signature. Take your time finding it.
