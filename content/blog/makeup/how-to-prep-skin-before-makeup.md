Great makeup starts ten minutes before the makeup. A little skin prep gives you a smoother base, fewer dry patches, and a natural finish that lasts all day.

## Start with clean, hydrated skin

Cleanse gently, then press in a hydrating serum while your skin is still damp. Hydrated skin holds foundation evenly. Dry skin grabs it in patches.

## Moisturize and wait

Apply a light moisturizer and give it a full five minutes to settle. This is the step most people skip, and it is the reason foundation pills or slides. Make a cup of tea. Your base will thank you.

## Prime only where you need it

You do not need primer everywhere. A small amount on the T zone or over visible pores is enough. Everywhere else, well moisturized skin is the best primer there is.

## Less product, better light

Warm one pump of foundation between your fingers and press it on rather than dragging it. Skin that looks like skin is the whole point.

A calm base routine means your makeup sits better, wears longer, and feels lighter. Prep is not an extra step. It is the first step.
