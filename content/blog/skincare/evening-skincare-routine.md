The best skincare routine is the one you will actually do at 11pm on a Tuesday. Here is ours. Two minutes, three gentle steps, no willpower required.

## 1. Cleanse gently for one minute

Massage a cream or balm cleanser in slow circles for a full minute. This is more than cleaning. It is the day leaving your face. Use lukewarm water, because hot water dries out your skin.

## 2. Hydrate damp skin for thirty seconds

While your skin is still damp, press in a hydrating serum with glycerin or panthenol. Pressing keeps the water where you want it, in your skin and not in your towel.

## 3. Seal in moisture for thirty seconds

Warm a shea and squalane moisturizer between your palms. Sweep it upward, finish at the temples, and you are done.

> Ritual is just routine that has learned to slow down.

Follow this simple night skincare routine for two weeks and your skin will notice before your mirror does.
