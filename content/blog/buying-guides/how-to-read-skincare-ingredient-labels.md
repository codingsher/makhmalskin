Ingredient lists are written in a language nobody speaks. These five simple rules translate almost all of it in under a minute.

## Start with the first five ingredients

Ingredients are listed by weight. The first five lines make up most of the jar. Almost everything after fragrance appears only in trace amounts.

## Water is not filler

Aqua at the top of the list is normal and good. Most great moisturizers are mostly water, held in place by clever emulsifiers.

## Long names are not scary names

Sodium hyaluronate sounds industrial, but it is simply a form of hyaluronic acid that absorbs even better. Judge skincare ingredients by what they do, not by how many syllables they have.

## Natural is a word, not a promise

The label proves nothing on its own. Poison ivy is natural, while squalane made in a lab is identical to the one in your skin. Look for the ingredient, not the adjective.

## When in doubt, count

A list with fewer than twenty ingredients usually means a formula that knows exactly what it is for.
