Healthy hair is mostly good habits repeated on wash day. Here is a simple hair care routine that works for almost every hair type.

## Cleanse the scalp, not the ends

Shampoo is for your scalp. Massage it in with your fingertips for a full minute and let the suds rinse through the lengths on their way down. Your ends get clean without getting stripped.

## Condition where the hair is oldest

Your ends have been with you the longest and need the most care. Apply conditioner from the ears down, leave it for three minutes, and rinse with cool water to help seal the hair cuticle.

## Be gentle when hair is wet

Wet hair stretches and snaps easily. Squeeze out water with a soft towel or an old cotton shirt, then detangle with a wide tooth comb, starting from the ends and working up.

## Protect before heat

If you blow dry, use a heat protectant every single time and keep the dryer moving. Better yet, let your hair air dry most of the way first.

One good wash day a week beats seven rushed ones. Slow down and your hair will show it.
