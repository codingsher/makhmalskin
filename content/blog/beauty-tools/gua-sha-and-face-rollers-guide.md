Gua sha stones and face rollers are the gentlest tools on the beauty shelf, and the easiest to use badly. Here is the calm, honest version of how they work.

## What they actually do

Facial massage encourages circulation and helps ease tension in the jaw, brow, and temples. That is the honest promise. Used gently and often, these tools leave skin looking rested and feeling relaxed.

## Always start with slip

Never use a stone or roller on dry skin. Apply a facial oil or a rich serum first so the tool glides instead of dragging. Dragging is how you irritate skin, and irritation is the opposite of the point.

## Keep the pressure feather light

The most common mistake is pressing hard. Your skin needs the weight of the tool and little more. Sweep from the center of your face outward and slightly upward, three to five slow passes per area.

## Two minutes is plenty

A short massage after your evening skincare routine is enough. Store the tool somewhere cool, wash it with mild soap after use, and it will last for years.

No tool replaces a good routine, but a calm two minutes with one can be the nicest part of your evening.
